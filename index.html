About Us

A Jalandhar trading house built on stock, price & reliability

Gumber Traders has operated out of Jalandhar, Punjab since 2002 as a wholesaler and distributor of cosmetics, skincare, hair care and baby care products. Led by proprietor K Kumar, we supply retailers, chemists, distributors and contractors across Punjab and beyond — with the stock levels and pricing that come from two decades of direct supplier relationships.
